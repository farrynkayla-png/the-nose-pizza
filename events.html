Questions

Event FAQs

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, 3–6 months is ideal as we're a small team and only take on what we can do properly. For smaller events, a couple of weeks is usually fine – ask anyway.

Where do you deliver?

Gqeberha (PE), Nelson Mandela Bay and surrounds. We're happy to travel further (Addo, the Garden Route) – just let us know where.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Tell us what you need and we'll do our best. Our kitchen does handle gluten, dairy and nuts.

Can we taste first?

Please do. Come see us at the shop and we'll put together a tasting.

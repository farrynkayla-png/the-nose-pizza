Loyalty FAQs

Do online orders count?

Yes. Order on our online menu as usual and show your card when you collect – we'll add your slice at the counter.

Do my slices expire?

Your card is valid for [12 months] from your first slice, so there's no rush.

Can I use my reward on any order?

Yes – claim it on any order when you collect. Rewards can't be swapped for cash.

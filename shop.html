Shipping & FAQs

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in South Africa. We dispatch within 2 working days; main centres usually arrive 2–4 working days later, regional areas in 3–6. We'll send you a tracking link once it's on its way.

How much is shipping?

R99 nationwide, free on orders over R600. Collection from the shop is always free.

Can you courier the pesto?

Our pesto is made fresh with no preservatives, so we sell it frozen and it's collection only. Keep it in the freezer, defrost in the fridge and use within 5 days.

Can I collect instead?

Of course – come say hi at 155 Main Road. Pesto and frozen pizzas are collection only so they stay frozen.

How do I order and pay?

Add what you'd like to your order, then tap "Send order on WhatsApp". We'll confirm stock and send you a secure card payment link.

Something arrived broken. What now?

Sorry! Send us a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours and we'll sort it out, no fuss. See our terms & returns for the details.

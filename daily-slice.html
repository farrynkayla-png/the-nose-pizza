The daily pizza word game · The Daily Slice

Guess today's 5-letter pizza word in 6 tries. A fresh one comes out of the oven every midnight.

How to play

Type any 5-letter word and press Enter. The tiles change colour to show how close you are:

  • B right letter, right spot
  • A in the word, wrong spot
  • X not in the word

Every word is something you'd find in our kitchen. Solve it in fewer guesses for more points, and play every day to build your streak 🔥 for bonus points.

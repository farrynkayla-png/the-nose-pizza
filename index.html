Our story

A family kitchen, not a franchise

Eppie Angileri – Dad – started The Nose in August 2020 as a little family-run home kitchen, making the classic Neapolitan pizza he loves. Gqeberha kept coming back for more.

How we make it

Simple food, done properly

[Swap in your own details – flour, how long the dough proves, where the ingredients come from.]

The dough

Mixed by hand in small batches and given time to prove, so it's light, airy and easy to digest.

The sauce

Made in-house every day from good tomatoes, olive oil and not much else. That's the point.

The toppings

Proper salami, real gorgonzola, fresh basil. If we wouldn't serve it at home, it's not on the menu.

The people

Every pizza is stretched, topped and baked by someone who cares how it tastes when you open the box.
